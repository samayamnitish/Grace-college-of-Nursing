About Us

About Nursing Career

The document presents nursing as an important contribution to preventing disease, promoting health, saving life and relieving suffering. It also describes opportunities for advanced studies, practice, administration and teaching.

Founder

Rev. Dr. S.S. John, Bishop started Grace School of Nursing to impart General Nursing and Midwifery training. The document states that the institution was upgraded as a College of Nursing from the academic year 2010–2011.

SGES

SARA GRACE EDUCATIONAL SOCIETY (SGES) describes its philosophy around the welfare and well-being of all and aims to help students develop integrity and contribute positively to society.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide high standards of health care, particularly to socially and financially deprived sections, and to train and educate people in compassion, concern and sacrifice toward professional excellence in nursing care, education, research and community services.

Our Vision

To be a leading and outstanding educational institution in Nursing, offering affordable education and contributing to the upliftment of society.

Symbol of Service

The supplied document describes nursing as an art requiring a compassionate and caring hand and as a profession combining service to humanity with achievement.

Facilities & Training

Clinical / Field Training

Students receive clinical/practical training through the Government Teaching General Hospital located near the college. Field training is described at Guduru and Pedana Primary Health Centers.

Nursing Laboratories

Facilities include Fundamental Lab, Community Health Nursing Lab, Anatomy & Physiology Lab, Biochemistry facilities and Nutrition Lab with hands-on learning and demonstration facilities.

Library & Seminar Hall

The library provides books, reference books, national and international journals, magazines, photocopy and internet facilities. The document also describes a seminar hall with LCD, multimedia and overhead projector facilities.

Scheme of Examination

The examination tables below follow the supplied document. Blank cells are retained where the source does not state a value.

Examination Regulations

  1. Applied Anatomy and Applied Physiology: 37 marks Applied Anatomy and 38 marks Applied Physiology.
  2. Applied Sociology and Applied Psychology: 37 marks Applied Sociology and 38 marks Applied Psychology.
  3. Applied Microbiology and Infection Control including Safety: 37 marks Applied Microbiology and 38 marks Infection Control including Safety.
  4. Applied Nutrition and Dietetics and Applied Biochemistry: 50 marks Applied Nutrition and Dietetics and 25 marks Biochemistry.
  5. Pharmacology, Genetics and Pathology: 38 marks Pharmacology, 25 marks Pathology and 12 marks Genetics.
  6. Nursing Research and Statistics: 55 marks Nursing Research and 20 marks Statistics.
  7. Minimum 80% attendance in theory and practical in each course/subject is required for appearing for examination.
  8. 100% attendance in each practical area is required before award of degree.
  9. Minimum pass marks are 50% in each theory and practical paper separately, except English.
  10. English and elective modules have a minimum pass mark of 40%.
  11. The candidate has to pass theory and practical examinations separately in each paper.
  12. If a candidate fails either theory or practical, the candidate has to re-appear for both papers.
  13. The maximum period to complete the course successfully should not exceed 8 years.
  14. All practical examinations must be held in the respective clinical areas, with one internal and one external examiner jointly conducting the practical examination for each student.
